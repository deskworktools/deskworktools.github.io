Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026 • Deskwork Tools
✓ 100% Client-Side Processing Files, resumes, images, and documents you process on Deskwork Tools are handled locally inside your web browser. We do not transmit or store your files on any remote server.

Deskwork Tools ("we", "our", or "the website") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how browser-side tools operate, and how third-party services and cookies are managed.

1. File & Data Handling (Zero Server Uploads)

The core principle of Deskwork Tools is local execution. When you use tools such as:

  • Resume Builder & Japanese Rirekisho: Your entered personal details, work history, photo, and signature remain in your browser memory and local device storage.
  • Image Resizer & Converter: Image manipulation takes place entirely within your browser's HTML5 Canvas element.
  • Document Scanner: 4-corner perspective warping and image filtering execute locally on your device.
  • PDF Compression & DOCX Conversion: Files are read and rendered using browser-based WebAssembly and JavaScript libraries (PDF.js, Mammoth.js). Your documents are not transmitted over the internet to us.

2. Local Storage and IndexedDB

To prevent you from losing your resume or draft progress if you refresh your browser:

  • Text Data: Non-sensitive form fields are saved to your browser's localStorage under the key deskwork_draft_v1.
  • Photos & Signatures: Uploaded image media is stored locally in an in-browser IndexedDB database on your device.
  • Control: You can clear all saved data at any time using the "Reset Form" button in the tool interface, or by clearing your browser site data.

3. Third-Party Libraries & External Content

To enable powerful features without a backend, the website loads trusted, open-source JavaScript libraries via reputable Content Delivery Networks (CDNs):

  • CDNJS (Cloudflare): Serves PDF.js, Mammoth, jsPDF, and html2canvas.
  • Google Fonts: Delivers typography (Fraunces and IBM Plex Sans) for readability.

These CDN providers may log standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) in server access logs strictly for security and content delivery purposes.

4. Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

Deskwork Tools displays advertisements through third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to fund the ongoing maintenance of these free tools:

  • Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to Deskwork Tools and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Log Files & Analytics

Like most websites hosted on GitHub Pages, standard web server access logs may automatically record basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and date/time stamps. This data is used solely for traffic statistics, diagnosing technical issues, and maintaining site stability.

6. Children's Privacy

Deskwork Tools does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since all tool processing is client-side, no children's data is gathered or stored by the platform.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, tools, or regulatory standards. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
