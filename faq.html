1. General Website Questions

What is Deskwork Tools?

Deskwork Tools is a suite of free, browser-based utilities designed for everyday productivity, job seekers, students, and office workers. Tools include a Resume Builder with 10 templates, an official Japanese Rirekisho generator, an Email and Letter writer, Image resizing and compression tools, PDF tools, a Document Scanner, a Multi-System GPA Calculator, and a Citation Generator.

Are Deskwork's tools actually free?

Yes. All tools are completely free to use. There are no paid tiers, no premium paywalls, no required registrations, and no watermarks added to your downloaded resumes, letters, or images.

Do I need to create an account or install software?

No. You do not need to register, log in, or install any browser extensions or apps. Everything runs directly in your web browser on desktop, tablet, or smartphone.

Can I use Deskwork on a smartphone?

Yes. The entire platform is built mobile-first with responsive layouts. The Document Scanner tool can access your phone's camera directly to capture documents on the go.

Can I use Deskwork offline?

Once the web page and its styling are loaded in your browser cache, the calculation and processing logic works client-side without continuous server communication.

2. Resume Builder FAQ

The Resume Builder helps job applicants and professionals assemble structured, recruiter-ready resumes by entering contact, education, work experience, skills, and regional credentials into an interactive form with real-time preview.

Open Resume Builder →

How do I use the Resume Builder?

  1. Enter your contact details (name, email, phone, location, and optional summary) in the Your details section.
  2. Add your employment history, educational qualifications, skills, and optional certifications using the dynamic repeatable fields.
  3. Review your formatting in real-time in the live preview panel.
  4. Switch between any of the 10 layout templates to find the design best suited to your target market.
  5. Click Download PDF to save your resume, or choose Print for your browser's printer dialog.

Which template should I use?

The best template depends strictly on where and how you are applying:

  • Compact (US/ATS): Clean, single-column design engineered specifically for automated Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) used by North American and multinational corporations. It avoids tables, multi-column blocks, or embedded graphics that confuse resume parsers.
  • Bangladesh Standard & Classic: Tailored to the expectations of Bangladeshi employers and government agencies. Includes dedicated sections for father's/mother's name, permanent/present addresses, national ID (NID), religious affiliation, and per-exam GPA.
  • Gulf / Middle East: Tailored for recruitment in UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and other GCC nations. Includes fields recruiters routinely request, such as passport number, visa status, driving license validity, and marital status.
  • International, Sidebar & Europass: Ideal for European, Asian, or creative job markets where a photo slot, skills rating meter, and clear sidebar layout are customary.
  • Academic: Structured for university admissions, research assistants, and faculty positions. Emphasizes publications, conference presentations, research interests, and honors over corporate employment.
  • Modern & Minimal: Balanced typography and spacious margins suitable for tech, design, marketing, and general professional roles.

What is the "Auto-draft summary" feature?

When you click the auto-draft button, a client-side helper reads the details you have already entered (job titles, degrees, and skills) and crafts a concise, professional summary paragraph that you can edit and refine.

How do I download or print my resume?

Clicking Download PDF generates a crisp PDF directly to your device. You can also click Print for your browser's native print preview with print-optimized CSS that removes UI elements.

3. Japan Rirekisho Tool FAQ

The Rirekisho Builder generates official, bilingual Japanese-standard resumes (JIS standard format) essential for Japanese employment, working visas, Specified Skilled Worker (SSW), and Technical Intern Training (TITP) programs.

Open Japan Rirekisho Builder →

What is a Rirekisho and why is it unique?

In Japan, employment and immigration authorities expect resumes in a strictly standardized structural format called a rirekisho. Unlike Western CVs that prioritize self-promotion, a Japanese rirekisho requires chronological accuracy, specific personal disclosures, and standardized bilingual fields.

What sections are included in this tool?

  • Basic Information: Name in English and Katakana Furigana, Date of Birth, Age, Marital Status, and Registered Address.
  • Physical & Health: Height, weight, blood type, eyesight (left/right), and health status standard inquiries for visa compliance and physical or hospitality roles.
  • Academic & Work Background: Strict chronological order of schooling and past employers.
  • Language Certifications: Repeatable table for Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT N1-N5), JFT-Basic, NAT-TEST, and English proficiency certifications.
  • Family Structure: Emergency contacts, dependents, and spouse details required by immigration sponsors.

How do I print or export my Rirekisho?

Click Download as PDF. The layout matches official Japanese paper specifications. You can also use the print dialog directly.

5. Image Resizer, Compressor & Converter FAQ

The Image Processing Tool resizes pixel dimensions, compresses file sizes, and converts graphic files between JPG, PNG, and WebP directly within the browser using the HTML5 Canvas API.

Open Image Tools →

What is the difference between resizing and compressing?

  • Resizing: Changes the physical pixel dimensions (width × height) of an image. Use this when a portal requires exact dimensions (e.g. 300×300 px).
  • Compressing: Maintains the pixel dimensions but reduces the underlying byte size by optimizing image data. Use this when an upload form rejects files larger than a specific limit (e.g. under 100 KB).

How does "By target file size" mode work?

When you select By target file size and enter e.g. 50 KB, the tool runs an intelligent binary search approximation across JPEG/WebP quality steps to produce a file that fits within your specified target limit without over-compressing.

7. Document Scanner FAQ

The Document Scanner turns mobile camera snapshots or uploaded pictures of papers, certificates, receipts, and handwritten notes into clean, perspective-corrected document scans.

Open Document Scanner →

How does the 4-corner perspective correction work?

The scanner lets you drag 4 corner handles to the four corners of the page. The tool then applies mathematical homography transformation to project the quadrilateral into a flat, straight rectangular page with optional B&W or grayscale enhancement.

8. GPA & Academic Result Calculator FAQ

The Academic Result Calculator provides dedicated calculation engines for diverse national and international grading frameworks.

Open GPA Calculator →

Which education systems are supported?

  • Bangladesh - Diploma in Engineering (8 Semesters): Designed for BTEB/Polytechnic curriculums. Supports calculating current cumulative progress up to any completed semester (e.g. 6 of 8 semesters) without demanding dummy future semesters. Supports credit-weighted and arithmetic modes.
  • Bangladesh - Honours / Bachelor's (4-Year): Supports year-wise, semester-wise, and course-credit cumulative calculations.
  • Bangladesh - SSC / HSC (5.00 Scale): Official 5.00 scale for Secondary and Higher Secondary boards, complete with 4th optional subject bonus rule.
  • Bangladesh - National University (NU): Calculates cumulative standing and maps results to official NU degree divisions (First Class for 3.00+, Second Class for 2.25-2.99, Third Class for 2.00-2.24).
  • United States - 4.00 Scale: Standard letter grade scale with configurable course credits and unweighted options.
  • United Kingdom - Degree Classification: Weighted module percentage marks calculating UK Honours classifications: First-Class Honours (70%+), Upper Second-Class 2:1 (60-69%), Lower Second-Class 2:2 (50-59%), Third-Class (40-49%), and Fail.
  • India - 10-Point Scale: 10-point CGPA calculation with standard conversion options: CBSE standard (CGPA × 9.5 = percentage), AICTE / Mumbai University formula, or custom multipliers.
  • Canada, Australia, & Europe: Standard 4.00/4.33 scales, Australian 7-point scale with HD/D/C/P bands, and European ECTS credit weighting.
  • Custom Grading System: Lets you configure custom grade names, min/max percentage brackets, and grade point mappings with real-time overlap validation.
