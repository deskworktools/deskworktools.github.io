Note: Grading rules may vary by institution. Choose the system closest to your academic program, or use Custom grading system if your institution follows different rules.
Which system should I choose? (Click to view guide)
  • Bangladesh — Diploma in Engineering: Designed for BTEB/Polytechnic students with an 8-semester curriculum. Supports calculating your current average up to any completed semester without requiring future semesters, with optional credit-weighting.
  • Bangladesh — Honours / Bachelor's: Designed for 4-year degree students supporting year-wise, semester-wise, or course-credit CGPA calculations.
  • Bangladesh — SSC / HSC: Standard 5.00 GPA scale for Education Boards with optional 4th subject bonus calculation.
  • United States — 4.00 GPA: Standard A+ through F letter grades weighted by course credit hours.
  • United Kingdom — Percentage & Degree Classification: Module percentage marks (0–100%) and credit weights, calculating First, 2:1, 2:2, 3rd, or Fail.
  • India — CGPA / Percentage: 10-point scale CGPA with standard conversion options (CBSE 9.5x, AICTE formula, or custom multiplier).
  • Custom grading system: Define your own grade tiers, percentage boundaries, and grade points with automatic overlap validation.

This calculator is for educational estimation only. Official GPA, CGPA, percentage, and degree classification depend on your institution's rules. Calculations run entirely inside your browser — no personal academic data is collected or uploaded.

Your Files Stay in Your Browser

Every tool on this site — the resume builder, the file converter, the document scanner — runs its processing directly in your browser using JavaScript. There's no upload step, because there's no server on the other end to upload to: this is a static site with nowhere for your files to go. Selecting a file, typing your details, and downloading the result all happen locally on your device. The one honest caveat: the page itself loads fonts and a few external code libraries (for PDF/Word conversion) from public CDNs when you open certain tools — that's a normal part of how any website loads its assets, and it's separate from your actual content, which never leaves your device.
